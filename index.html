Frequently Asked Questions

What do you mean by “men who love men”?

Ultimately, we mean men who are romantically, sensually, and sexually attracted to other men. Most of us have been called gay. Some of us say bi, or fluid. And some of us have never used a label at all. At Higher Love, we set the labels down and come as men first.

Who else will be there, and how do you choose?

Men who lead. Founders, entrepreneurs, and leaders who have built something, or are in the middle of building it. Every man here is a leader in his life, his business, or both. He also has a real personal growth and spiritual practice, and he brings it into his everyday life and work.

Every man fills out an application and has a private conversation with one or two of our founding members before a seat is offered. We’re selective on purpose. This isn’t about filling seats. It’s about protecting the room for every man who says yes.

I’ve never felt at home in all-gay spaces. Will I fit here?

Most of us would tell you the same thing, so you’re in good company. Many of us don’t frequent the typical all-gay rooms. The rooms we’re a part of have typically been entrepreneurial groups, men’s circles, or co-ed containers where we were usually the one who was “different.” Being into men is what we have in common. It isn’t the totality of who we are, and at Higher Love, it doesn’t have to be.

What actually happens over the 4 days?

Less than you’re used to, on purpose. There is a plan and an intention behind every day: group conversations, individual sessions, long dinners, time in the pool, and conversations around the fire pit. There’s also an element of surprise. You won’t get a minute-by-minute agenda, because the invitation is to surrender and receive rather than have everything lined up. You’ll have plenty of space to connect with the other men and to simply be.

What’s included?

Everything, from the moment you arrive. All you have to do is get yourself to Austin. Your lodging and sleeping arrangements, healthy and delicious meals with dinner prepared by a private chef each night, and every group and individual session on the schedule are taken care of. A few optional add-ons, like bodywork and IV drips, are available if you’d like them. Tell us about any dietary needs when you apply, and we’ll look after you.

When and where is the retreat?

The retreat begins Monday, March 8, 2027, at 7 p.m. with a welcome dinner, and ends Friday, March 12, with departures by 11 a.m. It’s held at a luxury private estate about 40 to 45 minutes outside Austin, Texas. If you’re flying in, plan to land in Austin by 5 p.m. on Monday, and book your flight home for 2 p.m. or later on Friday. We’ll share the exact address one week before the retreat.

Will I have my own room?

The estate has a mix of private and shared rooms. Private rooms are first come, first served, so the sooner you register, the better your chances. Either way, this is a communal retreat by design, and you’ll be comfortable wherever you land.

Can I stay connected to work while I’m there?

If you can put work away for these few days, do. You’ll get far more from the experience. We also understand that you lead something, and a call here and there may be unavoidable. There’s room for that. At certain moments we’ll ask everyone to put phones away. Think of it as permission to be fully here.

Is this a sexual retreat?

No. We also don’t pretend sex doesn’t exist. Sex and sexuality are held as sacred here, and we talk about them like grown men, without shame. They’re never the point of the room. Every man holds his own energy and asks before he touches. What you can count on is real affection between men that isn’t leading anywhere.

What’s your cancellation policy?

When you commit, we commit. Your investment reserves your place at this retreat, and we secure the estate, the chef, and every detail well in advance. For that reason, your registration is non-refundable.

We also know life happens. In a true emergency, we’ll review your situation case by case and may offer a credit toward a future retreat. Registrations can’t be transferred to another person, because every man in the room has come through the application process.

We are setting a higher standard for “men who love men.”
