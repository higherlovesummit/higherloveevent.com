Does This Sound Like You?

You’re in a lot of rooms, but never fully met in any of them

You’re a founder, an entrepreneur, or community builder. You love men, whatever the world has called it over the years: gay, bi, fluid, or nothing at all. You’ve outgrown the bars and the apps and the scene. And you’ve got a personal growth and spiritual practice.

If that’s you, you already know the feeling. You’re in a lot of rooms, but never fully met in any of them.

So picture a different kind of room. One where the business conversation and the personal one can happen in the same breath. Where every man there was chosen, so nobody has to perform to belong. Where you can set the armor down and be met by men at your level, the kind who give as much as they take.

Higher Love is that room.

Why Be a Part

Growth without love is just achievement

The deeper you grow, the higher you love.

It’s fair to wonder what you’d actually get out of this. The practical part of you should ask.

And yes, some of it is tangible. A conversation that unlocks something you’ve been circling in your business. A partner worth building with. An introduction or a referral you didn’t see coming.

But honestly, that’s the smaller part. What you really walk away with is a handful of men who know the whole picture of who you are, not just the version on your LinkedIn. The ones you can call at 11 at night when things are hard, and the ones who celebrate the win you can’t quite explain to anyone else.

For a lot of men, it’s the first time they’ve been in a room where they don’t edit themselves. Where they’re simply met, and held, for every part of who they are. For the right person, this becomes the room he looks back on as the one that changed everything.

If any of this is landing, apply and let’s find out if it’s your room.

Questions

Ask the practical questions

What do you mean by “men who love men”?

Essentially, we mean men who are romantically and/or physically attracted to other men. Many of us have been called gay. Some of us say bi or fluid. And some of us have never used a label at all. At Higher Love, we are setting the labels down and coming to the retreat as men first.

Who else will be there, and how do you choose?

Men who lead. Founders, entrepreneurs, and leaders who have built something, or are in the middle of building it. Every man here is a leader in his life, his business, or both. He also has a real personal growth and spiritual practice, and he brings it into his everyday life and work.

Every man fills out an application and has a private conversation with one or two of our founding members before a seat is offered. We’re selective on purpose. This isn’t about filling seats. It’s about protecting the room for every man who says yes.

Is this a mastermind or networking group?

Not really. Networking is a phone full of contacts and nobody to call at 11 at night. This is more like a brotherhood. Men at your level who actually know you, who’ll challenge you and have your back, in business and in life. There’s no guru at the front of the room. Everyone gives as much as he receives.

I’ve never felt at home in all-gay spaces. Will I fit here?

Most of us would tell you the same thing, so you’re in good company. Many of us don’t frequent the typical all-gay rooms. The rooms we’re a part of have typically been entrepreneurial groups, men’s circles, or co-ed containers where we were usually the one who was “different.” Being into men is what we have in common. It isn’t the totality of who we are, and at Higher Love, it doesn’t have to be.

What actually happens during the retreat?

Less than you’re used to, on purpose. There is a plan and an intention behind every day: group conversations, individual sessions, long dinners, time in the pool, and conversations around the fire pit. There’s also an element of surprise. You won’t get a minute-by-minute agenda, because the invitation is to surrender and receive rather than have everything lined up. You’ll have plenty of space to connect with the other men and to simply be.

What’s included?

Just about everything, from the moment you arrive. All you have to do is get yourself to Austin. Your lodging and sleeping arrangements, healthy and delicious meals with dinner prepared by a private chef each night, and every group and individual session on the schedule are taken care of. A few optional add-ons, like bodywork and IV drips, are available if you’d like them. Tell us about any dietary needs when you apply, and we’ll look after you.

Will I have my own room?

The estate has a mix of private and shared rooms. Private rooms are first come, first served, so the sooner you register, the better your chances. Either way, this is a communal retreat by design, and you’ll be comfortable wherever you land.

Can I stay connected to work while I’m there?

If you can put work away for these few days, do. You’ll get far more from the experience. We also understand that you lead something, and a call here and there may be unavoidable. There’s room for that. At certain moments we’ll ask everyone to put phones away. Think of it as permission to be fully here.

Is this a sexual retreat?

No. We also don’t pretend sex doesn’t exist. Sex and sexuality are held as sacred here, and we talk about them like grown men, without shame. They’re never the point of the room. Every man holds his own energy and asks before he touches. What you can count on is real affection between men that isn’t leading anywhere.

What’s your cancellation policy?

When you commit, we commit. Your investment reserves your spot at this retreat. Every single person we accept into the retreat passes several gates before attending. Our time will be spent gathering the resources and building an unforgettable experience for you. For that reason, your registration is non-refundable.

We also know life happens. In a true emergency, we’ll review your situation case by case and may offer a credit toward a future retreat. Registrations can’t be transferred to another person, because every man in the room has come through the application process.

We set the labels down and come as men first.
